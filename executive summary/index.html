Current Approach

Method We Are Using Today

  • Energy-based approaches — gyro energy and acceleration as fall signatures
  • Statistical approaches — sliding windows, adaptive thresholds, distributions
  • Combining both — crossing energy and statistics improves reliability
We combine physics-driven indicators with statistical robustness to improve detection confidence in real riding conditions.

UWB Ranging — 5/12

Parameter 1 — Max Speed mode

Config

  • UWB data rate: 6.8 Mbps
  • Target period: ~1 ms
  • Median filter: 1
  • Profile: FAST_DISTANCE_ONLY

Benefit

  • High temporal resolution
  • Many samples, tracks fast changes

Expected limits

  • More local jumps & noise
  • Higher risk of impossible speeds
  • Sensitive to outliers
UWB Ranging — 6/12

Parameter 2 — Outdoor Robust mode

Config

  • UWB data rate: 6.8 Mbps
  • Target period: ~30 ms
  • Median filter: 7
  • Profile: ROBUST_DETECTION

Benefit

  • Smoother signal, fewer spikes
  • Better continuity & field readability

Expected limits

  • Lower frequency
  • Fast variations less detailed
Trades temporal resolution for stability.

UWB Ranging — 9/12

KPIs Analysed

  • Distance std — global dispersion
  • Δdist p95 — point-to-point jumps
  • Peak / jump rate — samples above dynamic jump threshold
  • Impossible speed rate — non-physical velocities
  • Rolling MAD p95 — local instability
  • Bad burst max — longest bad-point run
  • Link reliability — overall quality score
  • NLOS / CIR — multipath & non-line-of-sight
Global std alone is not enough.
Two signals can share similar std yet have very different visual stability.
Red crosses = peak events counted in the spike / jump KPI
